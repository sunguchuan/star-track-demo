## Scope

Applies to Litho Scanner A1 (T-LITHO-01) after every preventive maintenance and after any lens or wafer stage repair.

## Requalification steps

1. Run the stage flatness check and confirm focus uniformity within 20 nm across the wafer.
2. Expose 2 overlay monitor wafers with the standard reticle and measure overlay on 9 fields per wafer.
3. Overlay error (mean + 3 sigma) must be below 3 nm in both X and Y.
4. Verify dose uniformity: illumination non-uniformity must stay below 1%.

## Release

If every check passes, the equipment engineer releases the tool in the MES. If overlay fails twice, escalate to the scanner vendor and keep the tool down.
