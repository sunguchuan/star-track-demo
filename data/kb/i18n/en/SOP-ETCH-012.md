title: SOP-ETCH-012 Etch Chamber Wet Clean Standard Operating Procedure
---

## Scope

Applies to scheduled PM cleans of dielectric etch chambers such as Etch Chamber B7 (T-ETCH-07), and to unscheduled cleans after particle alerts.

## Prerequisites

- No wafers in the chamber, and the related lots are on hold.
- Chamber temperature below 40 °C, and the RF power supply locked out and tagged out (LOTO).
- Cleanroom wipes, IPA, new O-rings and a spare focus ring at hand.

## Procedure

1. Vent the chamber with nitrogen to atmospheric pressure and open the lid.
2. Remove the upper electrode and focus ring and check wear; a focus ring thinner than 2.5 mm must be replaced.
3. Wipe the chamber walls, lid and electrostatic chuck (ESC) edge with IPA to remove polymer deposits.
4. Replace the chamber lid O-ring.
5. After reassembly, pump down and measure the leak rate; it must be below 2 mTorr/min.
6. Run 25 seasoning wafers to restore the chamber wall condition.

## Release check

- Particle test wafers show fewer than 10 added particles, on 2 consecutive wafers.
- Etch rate of 3 monitor wafers within 2% of baseline.

When everything passes, record the clean time in the system and resume volume production. The whole job takes about 6 hours.
