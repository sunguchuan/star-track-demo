title: SOP-ETCH-021 Etch Tool RF System Calibration Procedure
---

## When to calibrate

- Routine calibration once per quarter.
- Calibration is mandatory after an ETCH-RF-DRIFT alert, or after replacing the match network or RF cables.

## Calibration steps

1. Connect an external power meter to the RF output and measure forward power at three power points: 300 W, 800 W and 1200 W.
2. If the measured value deviates from the setpoint by more than ±1.5%, adjust the RF generator gain coefficient.
3. Run match network auto-tuning under plasma conditions and record the tuning capacitor positions; a capacitor position beyond 90% of travel means the capacitor has aged and must be replaced.
4. Reflected power at 1200 W should be below 20 W.

## Records

Enter the calibration results in the equipment history and keep the deviation data for 2 years as the comparison baseline for the next drift alert.
