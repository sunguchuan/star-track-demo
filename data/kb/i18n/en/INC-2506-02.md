title: INC-2506-02 Etch Chamber B7 Particle-Induced Yield Drop Incident Report
---

## Timeline

In June 2026, Etch Chamber B7 (T-ETCH-07) first raised an ETCH-RF-DRIFT alert (high reflected power), which was only acknowledged and not acted on. Two days later an ETCH-PARTICLE alert followed, and DRAM-1z lot yield fell from about 96% to 88.7%.

## Root cause

The vacuum variable capacitor in the match network had aged and its tuning position sat at the end of travel for a long time, which raised reflected power and destabilized the plasma. This accelerated polymer flaking from the chamber walls, and the flakes became the particle source. The wafer defect maps showed an edge-ring pattern.

## Corrective actions

1. Replaced the match network vacuum capacitor and recalibrated per SOP-ETCH-021.
2. Performed a wet clean and seasoning per SOP-ETCH-012.
3. Shortened B7's PM interval from 3000 RF hours to 2500 RF hours.

After the actions were completed, the qualification lot yield recovered to 96.5%.

## Lessons learned

RF drift is a precursor of particle problems: an ETCH-RF-DRIFT alert must not be merely acknowledged; RF calibration must be completed within 24 hours of the alert.
