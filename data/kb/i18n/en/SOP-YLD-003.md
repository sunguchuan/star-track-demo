---

## Severity levels

- Level 1: lot yield below the control limit (93% for etch); hold the following lots on the tool and start the analysis.
- Level 2: lot yield below the lower spec limit (90%), or 2 consecutive lots on the same tool below the control limit. Besides the hold, convene an MRB review to decide whether the wafers are scrapped, downgraded or released.

## Deadlines

- Within 2 hours: notify the yield engineer, the shift supervisor and the equipment engineer.
- Within 24 hours: submit the preliminary root cause analysis and containment actions.
- Within 7 days: complete the 8D report and update the related SOPs or runbooks.

## Roles

The yield engineer leads the analysis and the 8D; the equipment engineer handles tool inspection and repair; the process engineer evaluates the recipe and process window; the production supervisor handles lot dispatching.

## Closure conditions

The excursion ticket can be closed only when the qualification lot yield is back above the control limit and the containment actions have become permanent.
