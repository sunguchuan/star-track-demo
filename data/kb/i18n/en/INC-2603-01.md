id: INC-2603-01
title: INC-2603-01 False CD Alerts Caused by a CD-SEM Metrology Recipe Error
---

## Timeline

In March 2026, CD-SEM Metrology C3 (T-MET-03) reported CD-OUTLIER alerts for two consecutive days across 4 lots, and production was paused for a while.

## Root cause

After a metrology recipe upgrade, the pattern recognition template was not updated, so the measurement sites shifted onto adjacent structures and the measured CD was systematically about 3 nm too large. The actual wafer dimensions were normal.

## Corrective actions

Rolled back the metrology recipe and rebuilt the template, verified it with a standard wafer, then remeasured the 4 lots; all of them passed and were released.

## Lessons learned

Rule out metrology first on CD alerts: remeasure and check the standard wafer calibration records before tracing upstream processes. Metrology recipe changes must go through engineering change (ECN) approval.
