## Process parameters

- Main etch RF power 1200 W, allowed variation ±3%; exceeding it triggers ETCH-RF-DRIFT.
- Chamber pressure 30 mTorr ± 2 mTorr.
- CF4/O2 flow ratio target 1.5, warning line 1.65, spec limit 1.7.
- Electrostatic chuck temperature 20 °C ± 1 °C.

## Quality control limits

- Lot yield control limit 93%, lower spec limit 90%.
- Added particles per wafer (≥ 0.09 µm): limit 30.
- CD target 45 nm, allowed deviation ±2 nm.

## Maintenance intervals

- Preventive maintenance (PM) interval 2500 RF hours (since June 2026, previously 3000 RF hours; see INC-2506-02).
- Wet clean: once per PM, plus an extra clean after particle alerts.
- RF calibration: once per quarter.
